Market Simulation

Market Simulator

An interactive game: trade a portfolio through real historical market history — the 2008 financial crisis, the 2020 COVID crash and recovery, or a calm 2017 bull year — using real daily closing prices, not synthetic data. Buy, sell, and rebalance as the scenario plays out, then get a results screen with your return vs. the benchmark, Sharpe ratio, max drawdown, and a risk-personality read on how you actually traded through the drawdown.
